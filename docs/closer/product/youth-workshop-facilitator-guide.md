# YOUTH WORKSHOP – facilitator guide

**Updated:** 18 August 2026

**Audience:** schools, youth work, training, and workshop facilitators

**Release scope:** voluntary peer pairings aged 14–17

This pack is a light two-person icebreaker. It is not therapy, assessment, conflict intervention, safeguarding intake, or evidence of participation. The question bank is research-informed editorial content and has not been scientifically validated.

## Before the activity

- Use pairings aged 14–17 on comparable footing only. Neither participant may assess, supervise, discipline, mentor, or control opportunities or consequences for the other. Do not pair a young person privately with an adult, educator, assessor, supervisor, or carer.
- Offer a genuinely neutral alternative activity when the surrounding event is mandatory. Nobody should have to disclose why they pass, stop, or choose the alternative.
- Ask participants to use no name, a nickname, or initials. Do not collect pair names, spoken answers, Pass counts, completion, or screenshots.
- Explain that partners may pass any card or end at any time. Do not reward disclosure, completion, speed, or depth.
- Arrange for accessibility, language, hearing, vision, mobility, sensory, and reading needs without making a participant explain them to the group.
- Use a device and setting where both peers can see the screen without exposing it to the room. The pack does not create a resumable game, but ordinary site preferences and technical hosting logs may still exist.
- Explain the confidentiality limit accurately: CLOSER does not record answers, but a partner can hear, see, remember, or repeat what is shared. Never promise secrecy between participants.
- Identify the institution's real safeguarding contact and procedure before starting.

## Suggested spoken introduction

**DE:** „Ihr sprecht zu zweit über leichte Fragen. Die App nimmt eure Antworten nicht auf, aber eure Partnerperson kann sich an Geteiltes erinnern oder es weitererzählen. Teilt deshalb nur, was diese Person hören darf. Wir sammeln oder bewerten keine Antworten. Jede Karte darf ohne Begründung ausgelassen werden; ihr könnt jederzeit aufhören oder die gleichwertige alternative Aufgabe wählen. Verwendet keinen Namen, einen Spitznamen oder Initialen. Wenn euch ein echtes Problem beschäftigt oder ihr Unterstützung braucht, ist dieses Spiel nicht der Meldeweg – kommt bitte zu [Ansprechperson/Schutzweg].“

**EN:** “You will talk in pairs using light prompts. The app does not record your answers, but your partner may remember or repeat what you share. Share only what this person may hear. We do not collect or assess answers. You may pass on any card without explaining, and you can stop or choose the equivalent alternative activity at any time. Use no name, a nickname, or initials. If you have a real concern or need support, this game is not the reporting channel—please go to [named contact/safeguarding route].”

## During the activity

- Stay available without monitoring answers or hovering over screens.
- Never ask what a pair discussed, why someone passed, or whether both people completed the same number of cards.
- Never turn Q06 or Q10 into a role assignment, Q15 into an assessment, Q18 into an adopted group rule, or Q23 into a participant commitment.
- Allow pairs to stop, change to the neutral alternative, or request a different practical arrangement without public explanation.
- Do not use `PREDICT`, public performance, competitive scoring, random dares, or group reveal rounds.
- If discomfort or a real safeguarding concern emerges, stop the activity for that participant and follow the established institutional procedure. Do not investigate through CLOSER prompts.

## After the activity

Ask only optional product/process questions such as whether the wording was clear, the length felt suitable, or a card was difficult to use. Do not request the substance of an answer. Record no participant-level outcome, and do not treat completion as attendance, engagement, competence, or consent.

Before broad institutional promotion, this pack still requires safeguarding, educator, bilingual, accessibility, privacy, and moderated target-age review. Current design sources include [UNESCO safe-learning guidance](https://www.unesco.org/en/health-education/safe-learning-environments), [GDPR data-minimization principles](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj), and the [ICO Children's Code](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/the-children-s-code-and-children-s-online-privacy/) as conservative privacy-by-design guidance.
