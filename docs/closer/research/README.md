## Authority and change rules

- The primary integrated report is the current synthesis; the workstream memos preserve deeper evidence and design detail.
- RaDi explicitly approved implementing FR-018 as **POWER, BY CHOICE** and a 21-card FR-019 **SLOW BURN** sequence on 17 August 2026, then approved a sharper editorial pass across all three adult packs. The synchronized catalog and runtime are the authority for shipped wording. The frozen memos remain historical evidence records, not current consumer-content specifications. Implementation is not validation.
- Classic questions, translations, order, and route membership remain immutable.
- Future FR-020-derived changes must retain the same atomic approval rule and synchronize the authoritative catalog, runtime, content revision, and regression tests.
- The owner decision advanced both implementations before the memos' recommended external gates. Those named specialist, legal, accessibility, bilingual, privacy, adverse-event, physical-device, and moderated-session reviews therefore remain explicit release-risk debt rather than completed evidence.
- SLOW BURN now deliberately departs from the memo's narrower body-area boundary: the runtime may invite explicitly named touch at the chest or breasts, buttocks, and inner thighs, while genitals, anus, penetration, breath or neck play, restraint, impact, and surprise touch remain excluded. The implemented interaction also departs from the memo's high-friction masked gate proposal after real-device feedback: the app stores ordinary route progress only, while all wishes, changes, pauses, and stopping remain direct interpersonal communication. Those owner decisions do not close any of the memo's external validation gates.
- The same low-attention decision now applies to every adult pack, including Late Night. Frozen research and audit memos preserve the earlier independent-gate recommendation as historical evidence work; the shipped catalog and runtime supersede that interaction design with one shared introduction and direct interpersonal check-ins.
