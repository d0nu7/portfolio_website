# CLOSER full question-bank audit — post-research pass

**Audit date:** 17 August 2026
**Authoritative catalog:** `docs/closer/content/question-catalog.de-en.md`
**Scope:** all 432 master questions in 12 implemented packs; every DE/EN pair, curated route, question-bound response card and PLAYFUL action, Private Moment interaction, and pack finale
**Evidence base:** the citation-audited FR-018–FR-020 report and the FR-020 cross-pack memo
**Change status:** all 37 FR-020 entries and the three addenda were owner-approved and implemented atomically on 17 August 2026; validation gates remain open

> **Decision boundary.** `Retain` means that desk review found no sufficiently specific reason to change the item now. It does not mean that the question, translation, route, mechanic, pack, or product outcome is scientifically validated. Owner approval and implementation resolve the editorial disposition only; every named specialist, bilingual, accessibility, physical-device, and moderated-session gate remains separate.

## 1. Decision

The question architecture remains worth keeping. The bank is unusually disciplined about stable IDs, reciprocal turns, unconditional Pass, route curation, relationship plurality, and explicit specialist-pack boundaries. A wholesale rewrite would add risk without evidential justification.

The full pass produces this complete accounting:

| Disposition | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|

| Non-Classic wording changes implemented | 20 | Seventeen entries documented in FR-020 plus three cross-pack consistency findings below. |
| Question IDs with route, response-card, or PLAYFUL changes implemented | 14 | Base wording remains on some IDs; one of these IDs, Deep Q34, is also in the wording set. |
| Unique non-Classic IDs with any implemented intervention | 33 | Union of the preceding two non-Classic sets. |
| Other non-Classic questions retained | 363 | No atomic wording or mechanic change is justified by desk review; user and specialist gates still apply. |
| Total audited | **432** | Exactly 36 questions in each of 12 packs. |

There are no exact duplicate English questions, no missing bilingual cells, no missing Q01–Q36 position, and no pack with more or fewer than 36 master questions. The material problem was not bank-wide quality. It was a small set of presuppositions, obligation-like wording, ambiguous signal language, route clustering, and mechanics that could change how an otherwise acceptable question is experienced. The exact audited interventions are now synchronized across catalog, runtime, and regression tests.

## 2. Audit standard

Every item was assessed as the product presents it, not as isolated prose:

1. **Construct:** what the question actually asks, and whether the cited evidence supports only the general mechanism or the exact claim.
2. **Choice:** whether the item permits no answer, uncertainty, difference, non-reciprocation, and no future action.
3. **Presupposition:** whether it assumes closeness, shared history, successful coping, attraction, family form, ability, money, speech, reconciliation, or a desired future.
4. **Consent and obligation:** whether preferences, fantasies, positive responses, or prior choices could be read as permission, a promise, or a standing instruction.
5. **Power:** whether a nominally voluntary answer can become employment, academic, family, relational, or sexual leverage.
6. **Accessibility:** whether the item or its mechanic requires speech, hearing, vision, speed, memory, synchronized performance, or a particular body/mobility profile.
7. **Bilingual equivalence:** agent, modality, obligation, time horizon, intimacy, valence, and what counts as an answer in DE and EN.
8. **Arc and route:** intensity at the actual route position, semantic repetition, recovery space, and future-obligation clustering.
9. **Mechanic interaction:** response cards, `PREDICT`, `BOTH`, `NO THINKING`, `STAY`, Private Moments, finales, and handoff cost.
10. **Claims:** no inference that an exact item, route, translation, or pack is validated merely because a related mechanism has evidence.

The evidence basis and its transfer limits are documented in [FR-018–FR-020 deep research](./fr-018-fr-020-deep-research.md); the final citation review is [clean](./fr-018-fr-020-citation-qa.md). The prior 37-entry atomic register remains in [FR-020 Section 13](./fr-020-cross-pack-audit-memo.md#13-exact-proposed-bilingual-content-change-register).

### 3.1 Classic is locked, not risk-free

Classic Q01–Q36 remain immutable under the current product rule. That is a provenance constraint, not an endorsement of every item for every pair.

- Q07, Q18, Q19, Q33, and Q35 introduce death, terrible memory, loss, or acute regret.
- Q23 and Q24 assume conventional family/mother relevance more strongly than newer CLOSER packs do.
- Q11, Q22, Q25, Q28, Q29, Q30, and Q36 prescribe timed disclosure, interpersonal performance, praise, embarrassment, crying, or problem discussion.
- Full is the only route that closely follows the published sequence. Quick and Standard are unvalidated CLOSER extracts, and Pass, styles, Private Moments, and finales are product adaptations.

Required mitigation is accurate positioning, an explicit depth choice, persistent Pass/End, no outcome promise, and observed sessions. It is not rewriting Classic or calling shorter routes scientifically validated.

## 4. Three new cross-pack consistency findings

These findings arose from applying the consent and ask-don't-infer rules consistently across packs after the FR-018/FR-019 safety architecture was complete. They are implemented additions to, not replacements for, the FR-020 register. The `Current` and `Proposed` labels below preserve the audit's before/after record.

### `date-night-q03`

- **Current DE:** „Woran merkst du, dass jemand mit dir flirtet?“
- **Current EN:** “How can you tell when someone is flirting with you?”
- **Proposed DE:** „Wie kann eine Person für dich klar zeigen, dass sie flirtet, und zugleich nachfragen, ob das willkommen ist?“
- **Proposed EN:** “How can someone make it clear to you that they are flirting while also checking whether it is welcome?”
- **Classification / priority:** Rewrite both; P1 before pilot.
- **Basis:** Editorial safety inference from the report's internal/external-consent distinction. A perceived flirting cue is contextual information, not permission for closeness or touch.
- **Reason:** The current wording rewards inference. The proposal keeps the light flirting topic while pairing clarity with a direct check.
- **Gate:** Bilingual cognitive interviews and Date Night sessions; reject if the replacement feels legalistic or duplicates later boundary questions.

### `friends-q14`

- **Current DE:** „Woran kann eine befreundete Person erkennen, ob du gerade Rat oder einfach Gesellschaft möchtest?“
- **Current EN:** “How can a friend tell whether you want advice or simply some company?”
- **Proposed DE:** „Wie möchtest du gefragt werden, ob du gerade Rat, Gesellschaft oder etwas anderes möchtest?“
- **Proposed EN:** “How would you like a friend to ask whether you want advice, company, or something else?”
- **Classification / priority:** Rewrite both; P1 communication consistency.
- **Basis:** Editorial inference supported indirectly by responsiveness research; the evidence does not validate cue-reading.
- **Reason:** Family Q14 was already corrected from inferring support versus space to asking. The same rule should apply to Friends.
- **Gate:** DE/EN paraphrase interviews and ordinary-friendship sessions; verify that `something else` does not make the prompt too abstract.

### `old-friends-q22`

- **Current DE:** „Was fühlt sich zwischen uns noch immer mühelos an?“
- **Current EN:** “What still feels effortless between us?”
- **Proposed DE:** „Gibt es etwas, das sich zwischen euch heute mühelos anfühlt – und wenn ja, was?“
- **Proposed EN:** “Is there anything between the two of you that feels effortless today—and if so, what?”
- **Classification / priority:** Rewrite both; P1 relationship-presupposition fix.
- **Basis:** Editorial inclusion inference informed by the report's varied estrangement and reconciliation evidence.
- **Reason:** `still` assumes continuity and the question presupposes ease. The proposal allows `nothing` while retaining a present-day positive observation.
- **Gate:** Sessions with reconnecting and non-reconnecting pairs; compare direct `you/us` phrasing for naturalness without restoring the assumption.

## 6. Shell, Private Moment, and finale findings

The following non-question changes were implemented because they materially affect how questions are interpreted:

- Classic's blurb is narrowed and the Full-versus-extract research note is present. It does not claim love, compatibility, any-relationship applicability, or lasting change.
- Couples Act II is renamed from `REPAIR` to `ABSTIMMEN / CHECKING IN`; questions do not promise relationship repair.
- `Karten zeigen / Show cards` is now `Private Karten ansehen / View private cards`.
- The Road Trip Quick finale ends the round and repeats that anyone resuming driving must not handle or read the phone.
- Keep the approved pack-specific Private Moments, but they remain unvalidated on a shared physical device. No private free text or answer content enters state.
- Keep Late Night out of generic Q37 and all PLAYFUL/proxy/speed/touch mechanics. Its final reminder that answers are information, not permission, is required.
- Every preference, fantasy, boundary, support answer, future idea, and positive response remains information. It is never consent, a promise, a standing instruction, or proof of compatibility.

## 8. Priority and release logic

### Implemented editorial and runtime changes

- Late Night Q26/Q27 were rewritten and Q30/Q34 were swapped between Standard and Full.
- Couples Q28 no longer requires a promise, Q29 no longer uses `PREDICT`, and Act II no longer claims `REPAIR`.
- Old Friends Q13/Q19 and Family Q14 no longer rely on the audited closeness, coping, or cue-reading assumptions.
- Classic positioning and route labels distinguish the full research-derived sequence from CLOSER extracts.
- Road Trip Quick now has its route-specific safety finale.
- Every other exact wording, route, response-card, PLAYFUL, and shared-action entry in the 40-item register is synchronized across the catalog and runtime.

### Remaining release and validation gates

- Resolve Late Night wording and route behavior with consent/sexual-health and accessibility specialists before broad release.
- Complete shared-device, background/resume, screen-reader, keyboard, text-scale, and physical handoff validation for every Private Moment path.
- Review every implemented wording change, including the three addenda, through independent bilingual paraphrase interviews.
- Observe Pass/End concentration, question-level abandonment, route duration distribution, one-sided finales, and recovery after high-intensity items without storing answer content.

### Stop rules

Do not average away one credible coercion, retaliation, road-safety, privacy, or sexual-consent failure. Pause that path. Re-review any item that repeatedly becomes a promise, action request, compatibility test, diagnosis, disclosure demand, or proxy for institutional evaluation.

## 9. What this audit does not establish

- It is a structured evidence and editorial audit, not a preregistered systematic review or product validation study.
- No published study evaluates a CLOSER question, route, bilingual pair, mechanic, or complete pack.
- Related evidence supports mechanisms and design cautions, not exact wording or outcomes.
- The absence of a desk finding is not evidence that every user, language variety, access need, relationship, or context will experience the item safely.
- Approval, implementation, specialist sign-off, cognitive interviewing, moderated dyad testing, and post-release monitoring are separate gates.

## 10. Final recommendation

Keep the 432-question architecture and Classic's 36 questions unchanged. The 20 wording changes and 14 mechanic-linked IDs have been implemented atomically with synchronized catalog/runtime copy and regression tests. Treat all questions—including the 363 retained non-Classic questions—as unvalidated product hypotheses until the bilingual, contextual, specialist, accessibility, physical-device, and moderated-session gates named for their packs are completed.
